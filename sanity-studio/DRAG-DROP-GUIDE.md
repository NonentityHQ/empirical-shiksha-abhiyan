# Client guide: drag, drop and edit

- Open **Website Pages**, then choose the page (Home, About Us, etc.).
- Open **Page sections**. Drag the grip beside a section to change its order.
- Open a section to edit its heading, story, main photo, photo gallery, cards, timeline, FAQ, video or visibility.
- For photo strips, open **Website-wide Settings → Scrolling photo strips**. Drag photos to reorder; add or remove photos there.
- For milestones, open **Milestones & Badges**. Change hour thresholds, labels, descriptions and badge images; drag to reorder.
- For initiative galleries, open **Initiatives**, choose the initiative, then reorder its gallery photos.
- Press **Publish** to make the change live. Publishing content does not redeploy the public website after frontend runtime integration is complete.
